# 数字找一找 — 入学准备100字学习卡片 Prompt

> 分类：入学准备100字
> 主题序号：7.8
> 目标：在游戏寻宝场景中认识一到十

---

```text
**数字找一找** (主题): Hi Kiki

### I. Canvas & Style Lock
- Exact 1024x1024 square
- Premium toy-diorama counting game room or playground
- Bright, clear, not cluttered

### II. Scene Background
Create a playful counting scene where children find groups of objects from one to ten. Use ten small zones with clearly countable groups: one sun badge, two balloons, three blocks, four flowers, five stars, six apples, seven toy ducks, eight shells, nine buttons, ten small flags.

### III. Character Cast
Use Mimi + Yuki + Kiki. Jack is absent to keep counting zones clear.
- Yuki identity lock: the same approved 10-year-old elder sister, soft oval face, deep-brown eyes, natural thin brows, small rounded nose, gentle mouth shape, long flowing pure-black hair, and taller slender school-age body. Use a calm encouraging counting expression.
- Kiki identity lock: the same approved 4-year-old younger sister, full round face, slightly wide-set round deep-brown eyes, small round nose, soft cheeks, two warm chestnut-brown pigtails, shorter toddler body, and larger head-to-body ratio. Use a bright discovery expression.
- Mimi identity lock: the same approved white short-haired cat, round face, blue-green eyes, pink nose, and bell collar; use a curious searching expression.
- Preserve identical face geometry, eye spacing, skin tone, hair color, age, and body proportions across cards. At comparable depth Yuki is 1.35-1.45 times Kiki's height. Do not let the counting objects replace or visually redesign the characters.
- Exact cast contract: exactly 2 people (Yuki and Kiki) and exactly 1 animal (Mimi). No Jack, background people, or other animals.

### IV. 10 Target Vocabulary Objects
- [yī] / 一 / One (/wʌn/)
- [èr] / 二 / Two (/tuː/)
- [sān] / 三 / Three (/θriː/)
- [sì] / 四 / Four (/fɔːr/)
- [wǔ] / 五 / Five (/faɪv/)
- [liù] / 六 / Six (/sɪks/)
- [qī] / 七 / Seven (/ˈsevən/)
- [bā] / 八 / Eight (/eɪt/)
- [jiǔ] / 九 / Nine (/naɪn/)
- [shí] / 十 / Ten (/ten/)

Object placement map:
1. 一 — one sun badge.
2. 二 — two balloons.
3. 三 — three toy blocks.
4. 四 — four flowers.
5. 五 — five stars.
6. 六 — six apples.
7. 七 — seven toy ducks.
8. 八 — eight shells.
9. 九 — nine buttons.
10. 十 — ten small flags.

Critical placement rules:
- Each number must correspond to a visible countable group.
- Do not write Arabic numerals as large labels; use object count as anchor.
- Keep groups separated so children can count.

### VIII. Production Notes
- Can adapt existing number-recognition/playground style.
- Must avoid overcrowding; use clear zones.

### IX. Production Contract & Quality Gate
- target_count = 10; render exactly 10 countable groups, 10 label cards, and 10 arrows.
- people_count = 2; animal_count = 1.
- Every label card contains exactly three rendered lines: [pinyin], 汉字, English. Do not render IPA, titles, explanations, citation markers, numbering, Arabic numeral labels, or extra text.
- Each arrow lands on exactly one separated group containing the required number of objects. No object may be shared between two number groups.
- Run DIRTY TEXT SCAN, CLOSED VOCAB SCAN, COUNT CHECK, CHARACTER CONTINUITY CHECK, GROUP COUNT CHECK, MAPPING CHECK, ARROW CHECK, and VISUAL CHECK before accepting the image.
```
