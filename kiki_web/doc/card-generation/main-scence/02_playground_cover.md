# 游乐场景 - 一级场景分类封面 Prompt

规则入口：`main-scence/COVER_PROMPT_RULES.md`

---

## 基础规格

- 用途：一级场景分类卡片封面图
- 场景分类：游乐场景 (Playground Scenes)
- 输出格式：PNG
- 画幅：7:9 竖版
- 推荐分辨率：700 x 900
- 适用年龄：3-6 岁

---

## 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered main category cover for "Playground Scenes" for children aged 3-6.

Asset goal:
- App home category card cover, not a learning card page.
- Portrait 7:9 PNG, preferred 700x900 px.
- Full canvas usage with safe margins; no inner frame, no outer border, no blank edge.
- No text, no title, no label cards, no arrows, no brand badge, no citation marks.

Scene:
Build a complete playful amusement environment as a miniature toy diorama. It should feel like a safe, joyful playground space with slides, soft play shapes, paths, and outdoor activity cues, not a product shelf or toy catalog layout.

Theme anchors:
- Rounded slide, small climbing step, soft play tunnel, ball-pit edge, carousel-like toy element, or spring rider.
- Outdoor park hints such as trees, soft fence, path curve, sky accents, or play mats.
- 5-9 playground-related elements may appear, but they must form one coherent scene.

Mandatory character and pet rules:
- Exactly two cute sister girls must appear.
- Older sister: 10 years old, loose long hair, cute and friendly.
- Younger sister: 4 years old, two ponytails, preferably wearing a dress.
- Exactly one cute white kitten must appear.
- Place the older sister, younger sister, and kitten in three different depth layers: foreground / midground / background.
- All three must be visible and naturally integrated into the playground action.

Composition:
- Foreground: one large readable play object such as a ball-pit edge, slide step, toy wheel, or soft mat corner.
- Midground: main playground cluster with the clearest character interaction.
- Background: softened park/funfair anchors such as fence, trees, sky, or curved path.
- Keep movement lively, but preserve a clean layout for small app-card display.

Visual style:
- Premium miniature toy diorama.
- Rounded, solid, child-safe 3D forms with polished toy materials.
- Bright energetic palette: sunny yellow, sky blue, grass green, coral red, balanced with clean neutral surfaces.
- Soft daylight, clear contact shadows, visible foreground / midground / background depth.
- Fun, warm, and high-quality preschool education product feeling.

Hard prohibitions:
- No realistic photography, no flat illustration, no poster collage.
- No big text, no label cards, no arrows, no UI text, no logo.
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x], superscripts, placeholders, or metadata marks.
- No dark mood, unsafe traffic/danger emphasis, chaotic clutter, cropped characters, or blank borders.

Pre-final check:
Verify 7:9 portrait canvas, complete playground scene, clear depth layers, exactly 2 sister girls + 1 white kitten, no text artifacts, and strong readability as a small app category cover. Regenerate if any check fails.
```
