# 传统节日场景 - 分类卡片封面图生成 Prompt（RULES 对齐版）

---

## 📋 基础规格

- 用途: 一级场景分类卡片封面图
- 场景分类: 传统节日 (Traditional Chinese Festivals)
- 输出格式: PNG
- 画幅: 7:9 竖版
- 推荐分辨率: 700 x 900 (@2x)
- 可选分辨率: 350 x 450 (@1x), 1050 x 1350 (@3x)
- 色深: 24-bit RGB
- 适用年龄: 3-6 岁

---

## 🔒 借鉴 RULES.md 的硬约束（封面版）

- 必须是完整节日场景，不是节日物件陈列。
- 必须具备前景 / 中景 / 后景分层。
- 必须固定出现 2 个可爱小女孩（10 岁姐姐 + 4 岁双马尾妹妹）。
- 必须固定出现 1 只可爱白色小猫。
- 三个角色必须分布在不同景深层。
- 禁止大标题文字、引用标记、脚注标记。
- 禁止裁切导致角色或关键节日元素缺失。

---

## 🎨 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered category cover for "Traditional Chinese Festivals" for children aged 3-6.

Output:
- PNG, portrait 7:9
- Preferred 700x900 px (@2x)
- Full canvas usage

Scene goal:
Build a complete festive Chinese cultural scene with recognizable holiday objects (lanterns, mooncake-like shapes, zongzi-inspired props, red decorative accents) in a child-friendly way.

Mandatory character baseline:
- Exactly two cute sister girls: one 10-year-old older sister and one 4-year-old younger sister with two ponytails
- Exactly one cute white kitten
- Place them separately in foreground / midground / background

Depth and composition:
- Foreground: one iconic festive prop with strong readability
- Midground: core celebration zone and warm focus
- Background: soft architectural/cultural anchors and light decorations
- Keep visual rhythm joyful but not crowded

Visual direction:
- Festive warm palette (Chinese red, golden yellow, warm orange, cream)
- Rounded decorative 3D forms, polished and safe
- Warm celebratory lighting with soft shadows
- Cute premium diorama style

Prohibitions:
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x] annotation artifacts
- No giant text title
- No [cite:x] / [ref:x] / [source:x] or any annotation marks
- No replacing required sister+kitten baseline
- No dark or scary festive interpretation

Quality target:
Warm, celebratory, culturally recognizable festival category cover.
```
