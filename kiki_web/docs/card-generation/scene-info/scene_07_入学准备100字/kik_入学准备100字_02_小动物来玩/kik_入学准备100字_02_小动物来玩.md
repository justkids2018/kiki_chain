# 小动物来玩 — 入学准备100字学习卡片 Prompt

> 分类：入学准备100字
> 主题序号：7.2
> 目标：在院子/公园场景中自然认识常见小动物

---

```text
**小动物来玩** (主题): Hi Kiki

### I. Canvas & Style Lock
- Target age: 3-6 years old
- Exact 1024x1024 square, premium miniature toy diorama
- Warm park courtyard, soft 3D, rounded cute animals
- Full canvas used, no title, no frame

### II. Scene Background
Create a sunny courtyard or small park corner where Kiki and Yuki gently play with small animals. Use grass, a low fence, a tiny pond, a bird perch, and flower bushes to separate animals clearly.

Scene requirements:
- Each animal must occupy a distinct visual zone.
- Keep animals cute, non-threatening, and toy-like.
- A tiny pond can hold fish and duck; grass holds cow, sheep, horse, rabbit; branch/perch holds bird.
- Mimi is present as the white cat character, but the label 小猫 can point to Mimi.

### III. Character Cast
Use Mimi + Yuki + Kiki. Jack is not needed; keep the scene gentle and animal-focused.
- Yuki identity lock: the same approved 10-year-old elder sister, soft oval face, deep-brown eyes, natural thin brows, small rounded nose, gentle mouth shape, long flowing pure-black hair, and taller slender school-age body. Use a calm, caring observation smile.
- Kiki identity lock: the same approved 4-year-old younger sister, full round face, slightly wide-set round deep-brown eyes, small round nose, soft cheeks, two warm chestnut-brown pigtails, shorter toddler body, and larger head-to-body ratio. Use a bright curious expression.
- Mimi identity lock: the same approved white short-haired cat, round face, blue-green eyes, pink nose, and bell collar; show gentle curiosity toward the animals.
- Preserve identical face geometry, eye spacing, skin tone, hair color, age, and body proportions across cards. At comparable depth Yuki is 1.35-1.45 times Kiki's height. Do not redesign the characters or make the sisters look the same age.
- Exact cast contract: exactly 2 people (Yuki and Kiki) and exactly 10 animals (Mimi plus the nine other target animals). No Jack, background people, or extra animals.

### IV. 10 Target Vocabulary Objects
- [xiǎo māo] / 小猫 / Cat (/kæt/)
- [xiǎo gǒu] / 小狗 / Dog (/dɔːɡ/)
- [xiǎo niǎo] / 小鸟 / Bird (/bɜːrd/)
- [xiǎo yú] / 小鱼 / Fish (/fɪʃ/)
- [xiǎo tù] / 小兔 / Rabbit (/ˈræbɪt/)
- [xiǎo yā] / 小鸭 / Duck (/dʌk/)
- [xiǎo niú] / 小牛 / Calf (/kæf/)
- [xiǎo yáng] / 小羊 / Lamb (/læm/)
- [xiǎo mǎ] / 小马 / Pony (/ˈpoʊni/)
- [chóng] / 虫 / Bug (/bʌɡ/)

Object placement map:
1. 小猫 — Mimi the white cat near Kiki.
2. 小狗 — small puppy on grass.
3. 小鸟 — bird on a low branch or perch.
4. 小鱼 — fish visible in a clear tiny pond.
5. 小兔 — rabbit near flowers.
6. 小鸭 — duck beside the pond.
7. 小牛 — small calf toy-like figure in background grass.
8. 小羊 — lamb in another grass patch.
9. 小马 — pony near a low fence.
10. 虫 — cute ladybug or caterpillar enlarged on a leaf.

Critical placement rules:
- Exactly 10 animals/bug targets, 10 labels, 10 arrows.
- 小鱼 must be visible through water, not hidden.
- 虫 must be enlarged enough for recognition.
- 小牛、小羊、小马 must be visually distinct in body shape and color.

Each label card has exactly three lines: [pinyin], 汉字, english.

The 10 label cards must be:
- [xiǎo māo] / 小猫 / Cat
```
