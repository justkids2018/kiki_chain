# 认识我的身体 — 入学准备100字学习卡片 Prompt

> 分类：入学准备100字

> 目标：在照镜子/洗脸场景中认识身体部位

---

```text
**认识我的身体** (主题): Hi Kiki

### I. Canvas & Style Lock
- Exact 1024x1024 square
- Premium toy-diorama bathroom or dressing corner
- Soft mirror, wash basin, clean warm morning routine

### II. Scene Background
Create a child-friendly mirror corner. Kiki stands in front of a low mirror, Yuki helps gently, Mimi sits on a mat. Use the mirror and body pose to make facial/body parts visible. Avoid medical feeling.

### III. Character Cast
Use Mimi + Yuki + Kiki.
- Yuki identity lock: the same approved 10-year-old elder sister, soft oval face, deep-brown eyes, natural thin brows, small rounded nose, gentle mouth shape, long flowing pure-black hair, and taller slender school-age body. Use a patient encouraging expression.
- Kiki identity lock: the same approved 4-year-old younger sister, full round face, slightly wide-set round deep-brown eyes, small round nose, soft cheeks, two warm chestnut-brown pigtails, shorter toddler body, and larger head-to-body ratio. Use a focused curious mirror expression.
- Mimi identity lock: the same approved white short-haired cat, round face, blue-green eyes, pink nose, and bell collar; sit calmly on the mat.
- Preserve identical face geometry, eye spacing, skin tone, hair color, age, and body proportions across cards. At comparable depth Yuki is 1.35-1.45 times Kiki's height. The mirror reflection must preserve Kiki's exact face and twin-pigtail identity rather than creating a second different child.
- Exact cast contract: exactly 2 real people (Yuki and Kiki) and exactly 1 animal (Mimi). Kiki's mirror reflection is not an additional person and must match her exactly. No other people or animals.

### IV. 10 Target Vocabulary Objects
- [tóu] / 头 / Head (/hed/)
- [shǒu] / 手 / Hand (/hænd/)
- [jiǎo] / 脚 / Foot (/fʊt/)
- [yǎn] / 眼 / Eye (/aɪ/)
- [ěr] / 耳 / Ear (/ɪr/)
- [kǒu] / 口 / Mouth (/maʊθ/)
- [bí] / 鼻 / Nose (/noʊz/)
- [yá] / 牙 / Teeth (/tiːθ/)
- [liǎn] / 脸 / Face (/feɪs/)
- [tóu fa] / 头发 / Hair (/her/)

Object placement map:
1. 头 — Kiki's head.
2. 手 — Kiki's raised hand.
3. 脚 — Kiki's visible foot/shoe-free sock foot.
4. 眼 — Kiki's eyes, clear but not exaggerated.
5. 耳 — Kiki's ear visible from side.
6. 口 — Kiki's mouth.
7. 鼻 — Kiki's nose.
8. 牙 — visible smile teeth or toothbrush smile cue.
9. 脸 — Kiki's face outline.
10. 头发 — Kiki's twin pigtail hair.

Critical placement rules:
- Labels must not cover facial features.
- Use short arrows and place labels around mirror edges.
- 头、脸、头发 must land on different precise areas.

- 手 arrow -> lands on raised hand.
- 脚 arrow -> lands on foot.
- 眼 arrow -> lands near eyes.
- 耳 arrow -> lands on ear.
- 口 arrow -> lands on mouth.
- 鼻 arrow -> lands on nose.
- 牙 arrow -> lands on visible teeth/smile.
- 脸 arrow -> lands on cheek/face area.
- 头发 arrow -> lands on hair/pigtail.

### VII. Branding Badge

### VIII. Production Notes
- Recommended as a new original asset.
- Keep all body labels warm and playful, not clinical.

### IX. Production Contract & Quality Gate
- target_count = 10; render exactly 10 body-part anchors, 10 label cards, and 10 arrows.
- people_count = 2; animal_count = 1. The reflection duplicates no labels and introduces no new identity.
- Every label card contains exactly three rendered lines: [pinyin], 汉字, English. Do not render IPA, titles, explanations, citation markers, numbering, or extra text.
- Keep all face labels around the mirror perimeter without covering features. Head, Face, Hair, Eye, Ear, Mouth, Nose, and Teeth must have precise non-overlapping endpoints.
- Run DIRTY TEXT SCAN, CLOSED VOCAB SCAN, COUNT CHECK, CHARACTER CONTINUITY CHECK, MAPPING CHECK, ARROW CHECK, and VISUAL CHECK before accepting the image.
```
