```
**图书馆学习** (主题): Hi Kiki

### I. Core Setup
- Target age: 3-6 years old
- Format: exact square 1:1
- Resolution: exact 1024x1024 pixels
- View: slightly elevated 3/4 top-front view, looking into a classroom
- Style: realistic base with fuller, softer 3D volume enhancement
- Keep real-world proportions and recognizable shapes
- Do not over-inflate objects into balloon or gummy style
- Keep the image realistic first, but make forms feel rounded, solid, and full-bodied rather than thin or flat
- The final image must fully use the square canvas, with no portrait crop, no landscape crop, no inner frame, no extra border, and no unused blank margin caused by layout drift

### II. Real Classroom Scene

Create a complete, real classroom or library corner as if the viewer is looking into this room. The entire image IS the classroom — there is no separate display table or product shelf.

Scene elements:
- A wall with a blackboard mounted on it (back wall)
- A teacher's podium in front of the blackboard
- One or two student desks with chairs in the middle area
- Warm natural light from a window or reading lamp
- Bookshelves or a reading corner visible on one side
- The floor, walls, and furniture should all feel like a real classroom

Atmosphere:
- Quiet, focused, warm — like a small cozy classroom or library study room
- Apply subtle depth-of-field: center objects are sharpest, edges slightly softer
- Natural warm indoor lighting
- No huge title text anywhere

### III. The 8 Objects — each at its real-world position in the classroom

Place exactly 8 target objects in the classroom scene. Each object must sit where it would naturally exist in a real classroom.

**Object placement map:**

1. 黑板 (hēi bǎn / blackboard) — mounted on the back wall, large dark-green or black rectangular board. This is the biggest object and anchors the back of the scene.

2. 讲台 (jiǎng tái / podium) — standing on the floor in front of the blackboard. A teacher's podium with a slanted top surface, a visible front panel, and clearly taller than a student desk. It must NOT look like a regular desk.

3. 粉笔 (fěn bǐ / chalk) — sitting in the chalk tray at the bottom of the blackboard, or on top of the podium. A few white or colored cylindrical chalk sticks. Must be rendered large enough to be clearly visible even though chalk is small in real life.

4. 课桌 (kè zhuō / student desk) — a student's flat-top desk with a chair, positioned in the middle or foreground of the classroom. Horizontal surface, clearly different from the podium. 

5. 课本 (kè běn / textbook) — lying open or closed on the student desk surface. A textbook with a visible cover and spine.

6. 书包 (shū bāo / school bag) — hanging on the back of the chair or placed on the floor beside the desk. A children's colorful backpack with two shoulder straps.

7. 书签 (shū qiān / bookmark) — a colorful ribbon or paper strip sticking out from the top of the textbook (or another book on the desk). Bright color to make it easy to spot.

8. 眼镜 (yǎn jìng / glasses) — resting on the desk surface near the textbook, or folded next to the book. A complete pair of eyeglasses with visible frame and temples.

**Critical placement rules:**
- 讲台 must be visibly taller and structurally different from 课桌 — slanted top vs flat top, front panel vs open legs
- 粉笔 must be clearly separate from the blackboard — even though they are near each other, the chalk sticks must be individually visible
- Each object must be large enough to be clearly recognizable and tappable — even small items (chalk, bookmark, glasses) must occupy at least ~8% of the visible image area
- Objects should be spread across the full scene depth: blackboard on the back wall, podium in front of it, desk in the middle, some items in the foreground
- No two objects should overlap so much that either becomes hard to identify

### IV. Label Cards — exactly 8 cards

Each of the 8 objects gets exactly one compact label card.

Card body:
- Rounded rectangle, warm-white or soft-white base color
- Slight 3D thickness with soft highlight and gentle floating shadow
- Very thin, low-contrast border line in light warm-gray or very pale blue-gray
- Compact size — must not cover the main body of the object it labels
- All 8 cards must use identical style

CRITICAL — each label card must contain ONLY these three lines, in this exact order, nothing more:

Line 1: [pinyin in brackets] — ONLY pinyin, nothing else on this line
Line 2: 汉字 — ONLY the Chinese word
Line 3: English — ONLY the English translation

The 8 cards must show exactly:
- [jiǎng tái] / 讲台 / podium
- [kè zhuō] / 课桌 / student desk
- [fěn bǐ] / 粉笔 / chalk
- [hēi bǎn] / 黑板 / blackboard
- [kè běn] / 课本 / textbook
- [shū bāo] / 书包 / school bag
- [shū qiān] / 书签 / bookmark
- [yǎn jìng] / 眼镜 / glasses

**Label placement for scattered objects:**
- Since objects are spread across the scene (not on one table), each label card should float in nearby empty space
- Labels for wall-mounted objects (blackboard) can sit to the side or below
- Labels for floor-level objects (podium, desk) can sit above or beside
- Labels for small desk items (textbook, glasses, bookmark) can sit nearby without overlapping each other
- No two labels should overlap

Text color: all three lines use the same darkened scene-accent color family (deep blue-gray, gray-green-blue, or soft deep green-gray). Hierarchy only through font size and weight — hanzi largest and boldest, pinyin and english smaller but same color. Text must clearly contrast against warm-white card base.

ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION: Do NOT place [cite:1], [cite:2], [cite:3], [cite:4], [cite:5], [cite:6], [ref:x], [source:x], superscript numbers, parenthesized numbers, or ANY reference marker or extra text on ANY line of ANY card. Line 1 must be PURE PINYIN only. If any card shows cite artifacts or extra text, the entire image is invalid.

### V. Arrows — exactly 8 arrows

Each label card connects to its target object with exactly one arrow.

Arrow style:
- Slightly thicker than hairline, but still refined
- Smooth, clean, rounded body with small rounded arrowhead
- Arrow extends naturally from card edge with no gap
- Prefer soft gentle curves; use short straight lines only when clearer
- All 8 arrows use the same scene-accent color (soft sky-blue or low-saturation cyan-blue, never gray)

Arrow precision rules:
- Every arrow must TOUCH the body or edge of its target object, not hover near it
- 黑板 arrow → lands on the board surface
- 讲台 arrow → lands on the podium top surface or front face
- 粉笔 arrow → lands on the chalk stick itself, NOT the blackboard or chalk tray
- 课桌 arrow → lands on the desk surface
- 课本 arrow → lands on the textbook cover
- 书包 arrow → lands on the backpack body or front pocket
- 书签 arrow → lands on the bookmark ribbon or strip
- 眼镜 arrow → lands on the glasses frame
- 讲台 and 课桌 arrows must approach from DIFFERENT directions to avoid confusion
- 粉笔 and 黑板 arrows must clearly point to different targets — chalk sticks vs board surface
- Arrows must be short and direct — no wide detours, boomerang arcs, or crossing between arrows

### VI. Branding Badge
- Top-right corner, flush to edge, not floating inward
- One line only: Hi Kiki
- Warm-white rounded 3D card with slight thickness, soft shadow, very thin border
- Text color: fixed soft sky-blue
- Small, quiet, low visual weight

### VII. Hard Constraints
- Must be exactly 1024×1024 square composition
- No giant title or banner anywhere
- No unrelated decorative objects
- No unified recoloring of all objects — each keeps its natural color
- No balloon-like inflation
- No thin, flat, paper-like object rendering
- No cluttered composition
- No portrait poster layout, no horizontal banner layout, no large empty edge areas
- The scene must feel like a real classroom, not a product display board
- No incorrect label-object matching
- CRITICAL: absolutely no [cite:1], [cite:2], [cite:3], [cite:4], [cite:5], [cite:6] or any reference markers anywhere in the image. Every label card line 1 must be pure pinyin only.

### VIII. Pre-Final Self-Check

Before outputting the final image, verify ALL of the following:

1. DIRTY TEXT SCAN: Look at every single label card. Each must have exactly 3 lines: [pinyin] / hanzi / english. Line 1 must be pure pinyin. NO cite markers, NO reference numbers, NO extra text. If ANY card has cite artifacts → regenerate.

2. COUNT CHECK: Exactly 8 objects, 8 label cards, 8 arrows, no duplicates. Each of the 8 vocabulary words must appear exactly once — if any word appears twice (e.g. two 黑板 cards), the image fails.

3. MAPPING CHECK: Every card matches its correct object. 黑板→board on wall (not podium), 讲台→podium (not desk), 课桌→student desk (not podium), 粉笔→chalk sticks (not blackboard surface), 书包→backpack (not handbag), 书签→bookmark strip (not paper), 眼镜→glasses (not magnifier). Each word appears exactly once.

4. ARROW PRECISION: Every arrow touches its target object body. No arrow hovers in empty space. 讲台 and 课桌 arrows come from different directions. 粉笔 and 黑板 arrows point to clearly different targets. No crossing arrows.

5. VISUAL CHECK: Scene feels like a real classroom. All 8 objects are clearly visible and large enough to identify. Cards don't cover objects. Hi Kiki badge at top-right. Full 1024×1024 canvas used.

If ANY check fails → regenerate layout before producing final image.
```
