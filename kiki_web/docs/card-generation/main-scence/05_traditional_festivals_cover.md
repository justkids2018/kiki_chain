# 传统节日场景 - 一级场景分类封面 Prompt

规则入口：`main-scence/COVER_PROMPT_RULES.md`

---

## 基础规格

- 用途：一级场景分类卡片封面图
- 场景分类：传统节日 (Traditional Chinese Festivals)
- 输出格式：PNG
- 画幅：7:9 竖版
- 推荐分辨率：700 x 900
- 适用年龄：3-6 岁

---

## 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered main category cover for "Traditional Chinese Festivals" for children aged 3-6.

Asset goal:
- App home category card cover, not a learning card page.
- Portrait 7:9 PNG, preferred 700x900 px.
- Full canvas usage with safe margins; no inner frame, no outer border, no blank edge.
- No text, no title, no label cards, no arrows, no brand badge, no citation marks.

Scene:
Build a complete child-friendly Chinese festival environment as a miniature toy diorama. The scene should feel warm, celebratory, and culturally recognizable through objects and atmosphere, not through title text or a flat decoration board.

Theme anchors:
- Lanterns, round mooncake-like treats, zongzi-inspired props, paper-cut style shapes without readable text, red envelopes without text, festive table, or soft architectural cultural anchors.
- Warm family celebration cues, decorative lights, and safe rounded festival objects.
- 5-9 festival elements may appear, but they must form one coherent festive scene.

Mandatory character and pet rules:
- Exactly two cute sister girls must appear.
- Older sister: 10 years old, loose long hair, cute and friendly.
- Younger sister: 4 years old, two ponytails, preferably wearing a dress.
- Exactly one cute white kitten must appear.
- Place the older sister, younger sister, and kitten in three different depth layers: foreground / midground / background.
- All three must be visible and naturally integrated into the celebration.

Composition:
- Foreground: one iconic festive prop such as a lantern, rounded festival food tray, or soft red envelope stack without readable text.
- Midground: main celebration zone with the clearest character interaction.
- Background: softened cultural architecture, warm lights, window, or decorative hanging elements.
- Keep the festive rhythm joyful but not crowded.

Visual style:
- Premium miniature toy diorama.
- Rounded, solid, child-safe 3D forms with polished festive toy materials.
- Festive warm palette: Chinese red, golden yellow, warm orange, cream, with small fresh accents to avoid a heavy single-color look.
- Warm celebratory lighting, soft contact shadows, clear foreground / midground / background depth.
- Warm, culturally recognizable, high-quality preschool education product feeling.

Hard prohibitions:
- No realistic photography, no flat illustration, no poster collage.
- No big text, no label cards, no arrows, no UI text, no logo.
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x], superscripts, placeholders, or metadata marks.
- No dark or scary festival mood, cropped characters, or blank borders.

Pre-final check:
Verify 7:9 portrait canvas, complete traditional festival scene, clear depth layers, exactly 2 sister girls + 1 white kitten, no text artifacts, and strong readability as a small app category cover. Regenerate if any check fails.
```
