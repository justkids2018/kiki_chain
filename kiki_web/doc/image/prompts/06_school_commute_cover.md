# 上学路上场景 - 分类卡片封面图生成 Prompt（RULES 对齐版）

---

## 📋 基础规格

- 用途: 一级场景分类卡片封面图
- 场景分类: 上学路上 (School Commute Scenes)
- 输出格式: PNG
- 画幅: 7:9 竖版
- 推荐分辨率: 700 x 900 (@2x)
- 可选分辨率: 350 x 450 (@1x), 1050 x 1350 (@3x)
- 色深: 24-bit RGB
- 适用年龄: 3-6 岁

---

## 🔒 借鉴 RULES.md 的硬约束（封面版）

- 必须是完整上学路上场景，不是交通物件陈列图。
- 必须具备前景 / 中景 / 后景分层。
- 必须固定出现 2 个可爱小女孩（10 岁姐姐 + 4 岁双马尾妹妹）。
- 必须固定出现 1 只可爱白色小猫。
- 三个角色必须分布在不同景深层。
- 禁止大标题文字、引用标记、脚注标记。
- 禁止边缘留白或主体裁切。

---

## 🎨 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered category cover for "School Commute Scenes" for children aged 3-6.

Output:
- PNG, portrait 7:9
- Preferred 700x900 px (@2x)
- Full canvas usage

Scene goal:
Build a complete morning school-commute environment with clear context (street corner, school direction cues, backpack props, child-safe road elements), not a static product showcase.

Mandatory character baseline:
- Exactly two cute sister girls: one 10-year-old older sister and one 4-year-old younger sister with two ponytails
- Exactly one cute white kitten
- Place older sister, younger sister, and kitten in different depth layers

Depth and composition:
- Foreground: one strong commute prop (backpack, lunch bottle, or crosswalk sign edge)
- Midground: main school-route interaction zone
- Background: soft school/city morning anchors (trees, school gate silhouette, sky)
- Keep path direction readable and friendly

Visual direction:
- Bright morning palette (sunrise gold, sky blue, fresh green, soft red accents)
- Rounded toy-like 3D geometry, polished and warm
- Gentle morning light with clear contact shadows
- Child-friendly premium diorama look

Prohibitions:
- No giant title text
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x], superscripts, or annotation marks
- No replacing required sister+kitten baseline
- No traffic danger emphasis or tense mood

Quality target:
Warm, hopeful, and clearly recognizable school commute category cover.
```
