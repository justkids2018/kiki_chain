# 日常生活场景 - 一级场景分类封面 Prompt

规则入口：`main-scence/COVER_PROMPT_RULES.md`

---

## 基础规格

- 用途：一级场景分类卡片封面图
- 场景分类：日常生活 (Daily Life Scenes)
- 输出格式：PNG
- 画幅：7:9 竖版
- 推荐分辨率：700 x 900
- 适用年龄：3-6 岁

---

## 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered main category cover for "Daily Life Scenes" for children aged 3-6.

Asset goal:
- App home category card cover, not a learning card page.
- Portrait 7:9 PNG, preferred 700x900 px.
- Full canvas usage with safe margins; no inner frame, no outer border, no blank edge.
- No text, no title, no label cards, no arrows, no brand badge, no citation marks.

Scene:
Build a complete warm home-life environment, as a miniature toy diorama that combines a cozy living room corner, small kitchen hints, and child bedroom elements. The scene must feel like a real daily-life space that children can understand, not a display board.

Theme anchors:
- Soft sofa edge or cushion area.
- Small table with a cup or snack plate.
- Toy storage box, slippers, lamp, curtain, shelf, or window.
- 5-9 natural daily-life elements may appear, but do not arrange them like a checklist.

Mandatory character and pet rules:
- Exactly two cute sister girls must appear.
- Older sister: 10 years old, loose long hair, cute and friendly.
- Younger sister: 4 years old, two ponytails, preferably wearing a dress.
- Exactly one cute white kitten must appear.
- Place the older sister, younger sister, and kitten in three different depth layers: foreground / midground / background.
- All three must be visible and naturally integrated into the scene.

Composition:
- Foreground: one large readable daily-life object such as slippers, cushion basket, toy cup, or folded blanket.
- Midground: main home interaction zone with the two sisters and the strongest daily-life cues.
- Background: warm interior anchors such as window, curtain, shelf silhouette, lamp, or soft kitchen hint.
- Keep the center readable and the edge details simplified for small app-card display.

Visual style:
- Premium miniature toy diorama.
- Rounded, solid, child-safe 3D forms with soft polished materials.
- Warm home palette: light wood, warm yellow, soft coral, clean cream, with small fresh accent colors.
- Gentle natural indoor light, soft contact shadows, clear foreground / midground / background depth.
- Cute, clean, high-quality preschool education product feeling.

Hard prohibitions:
- No realistic photography, no flat illustration, no poster collage.
- No big text, no label cards, no arrows, no UI text, no logo.
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x], superscripts, placeholders, or metadata marks.
- No dark mood, messy clutter, cropped characters, or blank borders.

Pre-final check:
Verify 7:9 portrait canvas, complete daily-life scene, clear depth layers, exactly 2 sister girls + 1 white kitten, no text artifacts, and strong readability as a small app category cover. Regenerate if any check fails.
```
