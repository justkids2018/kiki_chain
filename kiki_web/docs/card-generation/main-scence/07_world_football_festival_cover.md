## 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered main category cover for "World Football Festival" for children aged 3-6.

Asset goal:
- App home category card cover, not a learning card page.
- Portrait 7:9 PNG, preferred 700x900 px.
- Full canvas usage with safe margins; no inner frame, no outer border, no blank edge.
- No text, no title, no label cards, no arrows, no brand badge, no citation marks.

Scene:
Build a complete cheerful children’s football festival environment as a miniature toy diorama. The scene should feel like a friendly opening moment on a small soccer field, with kids ready to play, soft stadium celebration cues, and clear football atmosphere. It must be a real playable scene, not a poster, trophy display, or object checklist.

Theme anchors:
- A rounded green soccer field with visible white field lines.
- One child-safe soccer goal, soft net shape, or goalpost curve.
- A readable soccer ball as a strong foreground or midground anchor.
- Festival details such as colorful pennant flags, soft stands, tiny cheering decorations, cones, scoreboard-like shapes without readable text, or gentle confetti-like color accents.
- 5-9 football-festival elements may appear, but they must form one coherent scene instead of being arranged like a catalog.

Mandatory character and pet rules:
- Exactly two cute sister girls must appear, using the Hi Kiki brand feeling.
- Older sister Yuki: about 10 years old, loose long hair, cute, friendly, confident, wearing sporty child-safe football clothing.
- Younger sister Kiki: about 4 years old, two ponytails, cute and energetic, preferably wearing a playful sporty dress or soft football outfit.
- Exactly one cute white kitten Mimi must appear.
- Place Yuki, Kiki, and Mimi in three different depth layers: foreground / midground / background.
- All three must be clearly visible and naturally integrated into the football festival action.

Composition:
- Foreground: one large readable football element such as a soccer ball, soft cleat edge, cone, or grass field corner.
- Midground: main football kickoff interaction with Yuki and Kiki, plus the clearest field and goal cues.
- Background: softened festival stadium anchors such as small stands, pennant flags, goal net, trees, lights, or sky accents.
- Keep the center readable and the edge details simplified for small app-card display.
- Preserve safe margins around all key characters, the soccer ball, and the goal area.

Visual style:
- Premium miniature toy diorama.
- Rounded, solid, child-safe 3D forms with soft polished toy materials.
- Bright football festival palette: grass green, clean white, sky blue, sunny yellow, coral red, with warm cream accents.
- Gentle daylight, soft contact shadows, clear foreground / midground / background depth.
- Cute, celebratory, clean, high-quality preschool education product feeling.

Hard prohibitions:
- No realistic photography, no flat illustration, no poster collage.
- No big text, no label cards, no arrows, no UI text, no logo.
- No readable scoreboard text, team names, country flags with real national marks, brand marks, or sponsor boards.
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x], superscripts, placeholders, or metadata marks.
- No aggressive competition mood, unsafe tackles, dark stadium mood, messy clutter, cropped characters, or blank borders.

Pre-final check:
Verify 7:9 portrait canvas, complete football festival scene, clear depth layers, exactly Yuki + Kiki + Mimi, no text artifacts, no real-world brand/country marks, and strong readability as a small app category cover. Regenerate if any check fails.
```
