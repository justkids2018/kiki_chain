# 入学准备100字场景 - 一级场景分类封面 Prompt

规则入口：`main-scence/COVER_PROMPT_RULES.md`

---

## 基础规格

- 用途：一级场景分类卡片封面图
- 场景分类：入学准备100字 (School Readiness: 100 Everyday Chinese Words)
- 输出格式：PNG
- 画幅：7:9 竖版
- 推荐分辨率：700 x 900
- 适用年龄：3-6 岁

---

## 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered main category cover for "School Readiness: 100 Everyday Chinese Words" for children aged 3-6.

Asset goal:
- App home category card cover, not a learning card page.
- Portrait 7:9 PNG, preferred 700x900 px.
- Full canvas usage with safe margins; no inner frame, no outer border, no blank edge.
- No text, no title, no Chinese characters, no alphabet letters, no label cards, no arrows, no brand badge, no citation marks.

Scene:
Build one joyful "First Step to Primary School" adventure as a premium miniature toy diorama. Show a continuous child-friendly journey from a playful kindergarten garden in the foreground to a bright, welcoming primary-school garden and open classroom doorway in the background. A small curved growth bridge or rising stepping-stone path connects the two spaces. The scene must communicate an exciting transition from kindergarten to primary school at first glance, while familiar everyday concepts appear naturally along the route. It must feel like the beginning of a happy adventure, not a study session, school-supply display, vocabulary wall, graduation ceremony, or collage of lessons.

Central playful event:
- The younger sister is happily taking one confident mid-air step across the colorful growth bridge from the kindergarten side toward primary school.
- The older sister is already on the primary-school side, turning back with an encouraging open-hand gesture as if welcoming her forward, without any speech bubble or text.
- The white kitten is playfully running ahead across the bridge, chasing one butterfly or a gently rolling ball.
- The movement must be forward and upward, never a sad farewell: lifted feet, fluttering ribbons or leaves, curved paths, sunshine, and shared eye direction make the moment feel celebratory and irresistible.

Theme anchors:
- Kindergarten foreground cues: one rounded play block cluster, a small slide edge, sandbox toy, pinwheel, or soft play mat; use only two or three cues so this side stays clean.
- One curved growth bridge or rising sequence of oversized rounded stepping stones forms the central visual route.
- Several stepping stones may contain simple embossed picture icons of familiar concepts such as home, family, kitten, bowl, ball, flower, tree, or sun. Use pictures only: no Chinese characters, pinyin, letters, or numbers.
- A tiny stream, flower bed, or soft grass gap beneath the bridge makes the crossing feel playful and magical rather than functional.
- Primary-school background cues: a warm open campus gate or classroom doorway, sunny windows, a welcoming path, low garden plants, and one child-safe learning corner visible inside, all without readable signage.
- A small backpack may appear on the younger sister as a supporting transition cue, but it must not become the main object or dominate the composition.
- Use 5-9 theme-related elements in total. They must form one continuous growth journey, never an object catalog, school-supply checklist, or literal display of all 100 words.

Mandatory character and pet rules:
- Exactly two cute sister girls must appear.
- Older sister: 10 years old, loose long hair, cute, confident, and joyfully inviting the younger sister forward.
- Younger sister: 4 years old, two ponytails, preferably wearing a bright playful dress, energetic and visibly mid-hop on the adventure path.
- Exactly one cute white kitten must appear.
- Place the older sister, younger sister, and kitten in three different depth layers: foreground / midground / background.
- Recommended depth arrangement: white kitten in the foreground running onto the bridge, younger sister in the midground crossing from kindergarten to primary school, older sister in the background at the welcoming school-garden destination.
- All three must be fully visible, naturally integrated, and emotionally connected through playful eye direction and one shared forward-moving adventure.

Composition:
- Foreground: the white kitten and a softened kindergarten play-garden edge with one bold playful cue.
- Midground: the younger sister's joyful crossing and the strongest curve of the growth bridge, including a few image-only concept stones.
- Background: the older sister at a bright, open primary-school garden or classroom doorway, clearly visible as the happy destination.
- Build a strong S-shaped, diagonal, or gently rising route from the lower kindergarten side to the upper primary-school side so the transition reads instantly at small size.
- Make the bridge-crossing action the dominant visual event; all learning and school props remain supporting details.
- Keep the center lively and readable and simplify edge details for small app-card display.

Visual style:
- Premium miniature toy diorama.
- Rounded, solid, child-safe 3D forms with soft polished educational-toy materials.
- Bright adventure palette: fresh grass green, sky blue, sunny yellow, peach, coral pink, lavender, and warm cream accents.
- Sparkling gentle daylight, soft contact shadows, polished highlights, and clear foreground / midground / background separation.
- Rounded bridge shapes, curved paths, fluttering leaves, soft garden details, and gentle toy-like scale changes may add wonder without becoming chaotic.
- Joyful, energetic, imaginative, high-quality preschool entertainment-and-learning product feeling.

Hard prohibitions:
- No realistic photography, no flat illustration, no infographic, no poster collage, no vocabulary wall.
- No readable text of any kind, including Chinese characters, pinyin, English words, alphabet letters, numbers, book titles, shelf labels, clothing names, school signs, logos, or UI text.
- No label cards, arrows, speech bubbles, floating icons, numbered markers, grids, or checklist arrangement.
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x], superscripts, placeholders, watermarks, or metadata marks.
- No giant book as the whole landscape, no static studying pose, ordinary desk-centered classroom, school-supply showcase, formal classroom pressure, exam atmosphere, graduation stage, sad farewell, or rote-learning imagery.
- No dense clutter, dark mood, unsafe jumping, cropped characters, duplicate kitten, extra cat, or blank borders.

Pre-final check:
Verify portrait 7:9 canvas; one coherent and instantly understandable journey from kindergarten to primary school; kindergarten play cues in the foreground and a welcoming primary-school destination in the background; one clearly readable growth-bridge crossing event with running, stepping, inviting, and kitten-chasing actions; forward and upward emotional direction without sadness or academic pressure; familiar life concepts embedded only as image-based discoveries; distinct foreground / midground / background layers; exactly 2 sister girls + 1 white kitten; 5-9 naturally integrated theme elements; no readable text or symbol artifacts; no labels or arrows; and irresistible readability as a small app category cover. Regenerate if any check fails.
```
