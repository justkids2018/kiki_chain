# 早上去上学 — 入学准备100字学习卡片 Prompt

> 分类：入学准备100字
> 主题序号：7.3
> 目标：在出门上学场景中认识常见物品、时间和动作

---

```text
**早上去上学** (主题): Hi Kiki

### I. Canvas & Style Lock
- Target age: 3-6 years old
- Exact 1024x1024 square, premium miniature toy diorama
- Warm morning light, home doorway and school-going atmosphere

### II. Scene Background
Create a warm home doorway scene in the morning. Kiki carries a schoolbag, Yuki stands nearby, Mimi follows them. A door, shoes, small car, umbrella stand, and morning sun create the story of going to school.

### III. Character Cast
Use Mimi + Yuki + Kiki. Do not add the mother or any other background person; the people count must remain exact and readable.
- Yuki identity lock: the same approved 10-year-old elder sister, soft oval face, deep-brown eyes, natural thin brows, small rounded nose, gentle mouth shape, long flowing pure-black hair, and taller slender school-age body. Use an encouraging morning smile.
- Kiki identity lock: the same approved 4-year-old younger sister, full round face, slightly wide-set round deep-brown eyes, small round nose, soft cheeks, two warm chestnut-brown pigtails, shorter toddler body, and larger head-to-body ratio. Use a bright, slightly excited school-morning expression.
- Mimi identity lock: the same approved white short-haired cat, round face, blue-green eyes, pink nose, and bell collar; use a curious following expression.
- Preserve identical face geometry, eye spacing, skin tone, hair color, age, and body proportions across cards. At comparable depth Yuki is 1.35-1.45 times Kiki's height.
- Exact cast contract: exactly 2 people (Yuki and Kiki) and exactly 1 animal (Mimi). No other people or animals.

### IV. 10 Target Vocabulary Objects
- [shū bāo] / 书包 / Schoolbag (/ˈskuːlbæɡ/)
- [mén] / 门 / Door (/dɔːr/)
- [xié] / 鞋 / Shoes (/ʃuːz/)
- [chē] / 车 / Car (/kɑːr/)
- [yǔ sǎn] / 雨伞 / Umbrella (/ʌmˈbrelə/)
- [zǎo] / 早 / Morning (/ˈmɔːrnɪŋ/)
- [lái] / 来 / Come (/kʌm/)
- [qù] / 去 / Go (/ɡoʊ/)
- [zǒu] / 走 / Walk (/wɔːk/)
- [wǒ] / 我 / Me (/miː/)

Object placement map:
1. 书包 — Kiki's clearly visible schoolbag.
2. 门 — large home door behind the children.
3. 鞋 — pair of shoes near the doorway.
4. 车 — small toy-like school car outside.
5. 雨伞 — umbrella in a stand near the door.
6. 早 — morning sun icon or soft sunrise clock near window.
7. 来 — small path arrow or gesture of Mimi coming toward Kiki.
8. 去 — visible walkway direction toward school gate.
9. 走 — Kiki's walking pose with one foot forward.
10. 我 — Kiki as the self anchor, label points to Kiki.

Critical placement rules:
- Action words must be anchored to visible movement or directional signs.
- 早 must not be a floating abstract word; use sun/clock visual anchor.
- 来 and 去 labels must point to clear direction/action anchors, not random empty space.
```
