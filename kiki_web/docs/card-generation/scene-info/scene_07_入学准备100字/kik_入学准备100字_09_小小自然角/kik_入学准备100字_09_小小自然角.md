# 小小自然角 — 入学准备100字学习卡片 Prompt

> 分类：入学准备100字
> 主题序号：7.9
> 目标：在校园花园/公园自然场景中认识基础自然词

---

```text
**小小自然角** (主题): Hi Kiki

### I. Canvas & Style Lock
- Exact 1024x1024 square
- Premium miniature toy garden
- Gentle outdoor light, soft colors, child-friendly nature

### II. Scene Background
Create a small school garden or park nature corner. Children observe sky, sun, moon decoration, clouds, rain drops, wind ribbon, hill, stones, flowers, and tree. Since sun and moon cannot both be literal in the same daytime sky, use a moon-shaped garden ornament or picture card.

### III. Character Cast
Use Mimi + Yuki + Kiki. Keep the scene calm and observational.
- Yuki identity lock: the same approved 10-year-old elder sister, soft oval face, deep-brown eyes, natural thin brows, small rounded nose, gentle mouth shape, long flowing pure-black hair, and taller slender school-age body. Use a quiet attentive expression.
- Kiki identity lock: the same approved 4-year-old younger sister, full round face, slightly wide-set round deep-brown eyes, small round nose, soft cheeks, two warm chestnut-brown pigtails, shorter toddler body, and larger head-to-body ratio. Use a gentle curious nature-observation expression.
- Mimi identity lock: the same approved white short-haired cat, round face, blue-green eyes, pink nose, and bell collar; use a calm alert expression.
- Preserve identical face geometry, eye spacing, skin tone, hair color, age, and body proportions across cards. At comparable depth Yuki is 1.35-1.45 times Kiki's height. Outdoor light may change shading but must not change skin tone, eye color, hair color, or facial identity.
- Exact cast contract: exactly 2 people (Yuki and Kiki) and exactly 1 animal (Mimi). No other people or animals.

### IV. 10 Target Vocabulary Objects
- [tiān] / 天 / Sky (/skaɪ/)
- [tài yáng] / 太阳 / Sun (/sʌn/)
- [yuè liang] / 月亮 / Moon (/muːn/)
- [yún] / 云 / Cloud (/klaʊd/)
- [yǔ] / 雨 / Rain (/reɪn/)
- [fēng] / 风 / Wind (/wɪnd/)
- [shān] / 山 / Hill (/hɪl/)
- [shí] / 石 / Stone (/stoʊn/)
- [huā] / 花 / Flower (/ˈflaʊər/)
- [shù] / 树 / Tree (/triː/)

Object placement map:
1. 天 — open sky area.
2. 太阳 — warm sun in sky.
3. 月亮 — moon-shaped garden ornament or wall picture.
4. 云 — soft cloud.
5. 雨 — tiny rain cloud corner or watering-rain visual.
6. 风 — wind ribbon/windmill showing wind.
7. 山 — small hill in background.
8. 石 — stones along path.
9. 花 — flowers in foreground.
10. 树 — tree on one side.

Critical placement rules:
- 月亮 must be a visible ornament/card if the scene is daytime.
- 风 must use a visual anchor like ribbon or windmill.
- 天 label points to sky, not sun or cloud.

### V. Label Cards
- [tiān] / 天 / Sky
- [tài yáng] / 太阳 / Sun
- [yuè liang] / 月亮 / Moon

- [shān] / 山 / Hill

- [shù] / 树 / Tree

### VI. Arrows
- 天 arrow -> lands on open sky area.
- 太阳 arrow -> lands on sun.
- 月亮 arrow -> lands on moon ornament/card.
- 云 arrow -> lands on cloud.
- 雨 arrow -> lands on raindrops.
- 风 arrow -> lands on wind ribbon/windmill.
- 山 arrow -> lands on hill.
- 石 arrow -> lands on stone.
- 花 arrow -> lands on flower.
- 树 arrow -> lands on tree trunk/crown.

### VII. Branding Badge
- Top-right corner: Hi Kiki

### VIII. Production Notes
- Can adapt existing campus garden card.
- Keep nature elements large and clear.

### IX. Production Contract & Quality Gate
- target_count = 10; render exactly 10 nature anchors, 10 label cards, and 10 arrows.
- people_count = 2; animal_count = 1.
- Every label card contains exactly three rendered lines: [pinyin], 汉字, English. Do not render IPA, titles, explanations, citation markers, numbering, or extra text.
- Sky, Sun, Cloud, and Rain must be distinct anchors. Moon remains a garden ornament in the daytime scene; Wind uses only the ribbon or windmill anchor.
- Run DIRTY TEXT SCAN, CLOSED VOCAB SCAN, COUNT CHECK, CHARACTER CONTINUITY CHECK, MAPPING CHECK, ARROW CHECK, and VISUAL CHECK before accepting the image.
```
