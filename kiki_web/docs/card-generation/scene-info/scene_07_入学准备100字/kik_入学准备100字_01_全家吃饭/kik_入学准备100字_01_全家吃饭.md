```text
**全家吃饭** (主题): Hi Kiki

### I. Canvas & Style Lock
- Target age: 3-6 years old
- Format: exact square 1:1
- Resolution: exact 1024x1024 pixels
- View: slightly elevated 3/4 top-front view
- Render as a premium miniature toy diorama, warm family dinner style
- Full canvas used, no frame, no large blank margin, no big title text
- Soft polished 3D materials, rounded toy-like people and objects
- Warm light, cozy home atmosphere, child-friendly and readable

### II. Scene Background
Create a cozy family dining room where family members sit around a round table. The table has simple bowls, rice, vegetables, cups, and warm home decorations. The learning focus is family roles, not food vocabulary.

Scene requirements:
- One dining table as the center anchor.
- Family members must be visually separated enough for labels.
- Use gentle home colors: cream white, warm wood, soft yellow, light green.
- Do not make the scene crowded; every labeled person/idea needs a clear arrow landing point.
- The word “家” should be anchored by a visible home-shaped wall decoration or a small house icon on a shelf.

### III. Character Cast
Use Mimi + Yuki + Kiki. Other family members are allowed because this is a family-role vocabulary card, but keep them toy-like and simple.
- Mimi: one white cat, clearly visible near the table.
- Yuki: elder sister, naturally part of the family dinner.
- Kiki: younger sister, naturally part of the family dinner.
- Additional family members: father, mother, grandparents, brother, baby, only as target family roles.

### IV. 10 Target Vocabulary Objects
- [bà ba] / 爸爸 / Dad (/dæd/)
- [mā ma] / 妈妈 / Mom (/mɑːm/)
- [yé ye] / 爷爷 / Grandpa (/ˈɡrænpɑː/)
- [nǎi nai] / 奶奶 / Grandma (/ˈɡrænmɑː/)
- [gē ge] / 哥哥 / Older Brother (/ˈoʊldər ˈbrʌðər/)
- [jiě jie] / 姐姐 / Older Sister (/ˈoʊldər ˈsɪstər/)
- [dì di] / 弟弟 / Younger Brother (/ˈjʌŋɡər ˈbrʌðər/)
- [mèi mei] / 妹妹 / Younger Sister (/ˈjʌŋɡər ˈsɪstər/)
- [bǎo bao] / 宝宝 / Baby (/ˈbeɪbi/)
- [jiā] / 家 / Home (/hoʊm/)

Object placement map:
1. 爸爸 — seated on one side of the table, warm smile, clear adult male figure.
2. 妈妈 — seated near Kiki, serving food gently.
3. 爷爷 — elderly man with gray hair, seated at the table.
4. 奶奶 — elderly woman with gray hair, seated at the table.
5. 哥哥 — older boy child, slightly taller than Kiki.
6. 姐姐 — Yuki, elder sister figure.
7. 弟弟 — younger boy child beside the table.
8. 妹妹 — Kiki, younger sister figure.
9. 宝宝 — baby sitting safely in a child chair.
10. 家 — small house-shaped wall decoration or shelf ornament, not a person.

Critical placement rules:
- Exactly 10 target anchors, 10 label cards, 10 arrows.
- Family labels point to the correct person body, not food or chair.
- 家 label points to the house-shaped decoration, not the whole background.
- Keep arrows short and avoid crossing over faces.

### V. Label Cards
Each target gets one compact floating label card with exactly three lines:
Line 1: [pinyin]
Line 2: 汉字
Line 3: english

The 10 label cards must be:
- [bà ba] / 爸爸 / Dad
- [mā ma] / 妈妈 / Mom
- [yé ye] / 爷爷 / Grandpa
- [nǎi nai] / 奶奶 / Grandma
- [gē ge] / 哥哥 / Older Brother
- [jiě jie] / 姐姐 / Older Sister
- [dì di] / 弟弟 / Younger Brother
- [mèi mei] / 妹妹 / Younger Sister
- [bǎo bao] / 宝宝 / Baby
- [jiā] / 家 / Home

### VI. Arrows
Arrow precision map:
- 爸爸 arrow -> lands on dad's upper body.
- 妈妈 arrow -> lands on mom's upper body.
- 爷爷 arrow -> lands on grandpa's upper body.
- 奶奶 arrow -> lands on grandma's upper body.
- 哥哥 arrow -> lands on older brother's upper body.
- 姐姐 arrow -> lands on Yuki's upper body.
- 弟弟 arrow -> lands on younger brother's upper body.
- 妹妹 arrow -> lands on Kiki's upper body.
- 宝宝 arrow -> lands on baby body or baby chair area.
- 家 arrow -> lands on the house-shaped decoration.

### VII. Branding Badge
- Top-right corner, one line only: Hi Kiki

### VIII. Hard Constraints
- No unrelated extra family labels.
- No text other than label cards and Hi Kiki badge.
- Family members must be friendly, clear, and non-realistic toy-diorama style.

### IX. Production Notes
- This card is recommended as a new original asset.
- Do not reuse high-complexity school card vocabulary for this card.
```
