# 字母认知场景 - 一级场景分类封面 Prompt

规则入口：`main-scence/COVER_PROMPT_RULES.md`

---

## 基础规格

- 用途：一级场景分类卡片封面图
- 场景分类：字母认知 (Letter Recognition Scenes)
- 输出格式：PNG
- 画幅：7:9 竖版
- 推荐分辨率：700 x 900
- 适用年龄：3-6 岁

---

## 完整生成 Prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D rendered main category cover for "Letter Recognition Scenes" for children aged 3-6.

Asset goal:
- App home category card cover, not a learning card page.
- Portrait 7:9 PNG, preferred 700x900 px.
- Full canvas usage with safe margins; no inner frame, no outer border, no blank edge.
- No text labels, no title, no label cards, no arrows, no brand badge, no citation marks.

Scene:
Build a complete alphabet-learning environment as a miniature toy diorama. The scene should show children exploring playful 3D letters and phonics toys in a real educational space, not a flat poster board or alphabet collage.

Theme anchors:
- Large rounded 3D letter blocks or foam letters.
- Phonics props, matching trays, alphabet puzzle mat, low shelf, soft board without readable words, or small learning table.
- 5-9 alphabet-learning elements may appear, but they must form one coherent learning scene.

Mandatory character and pet rules:
- Exactly two cute sister girls must appear.
- Older sister: 10 years old, loose long hair, cute and friendly.
- Younger sister: 4 years old, two ponytails, preferably wearing a dress.
- Exactly one cute white kitten must appear.
- Place the older sister, younger sister, and kitten in three different depth layers: foreground / midground / background.
- All three must be visible and naturally integrated into the letter-learning activity.

Composition:
- Foreground: one large readable letter or phonics toy object.
- Midground: main alphabet activity zone with the clearest character interaction.
- Background: soft classroom anchors such as shelf, wall board, rug, window, or learning corner.
- Keep the alphabet cue strong while avoiding a poster-like wall of text.

Visual style:
- Premium miniature toy diorama.
- Rounded, solid, child-safe 3D forms with polished educational toy materials.
- Soft bright palette: pastel blue, mint, peach, light yellow, balanced with clean neutral surfaces.
- Soft daylight, clear contact shadows, visible foreground / midground / background depth.
- Gentle, playful, high-quality preschool education product feeling.

Hard prohibitions:
- No realistic photography, no flat illustration, no poster collage.
- No big text, no label cards, no arrows, no UI text, no logo.
- No [cite:x], [ref:x], [source:x], superscripts, placeholders, or metadata marks.
- No over-complex clutter, cropped characters, or blank borders.

Pre-final check:
Verify 7:9 portrait canvas, complete letter-learning scene, clear depth layers, exactly 2 sister girls + 1 white kitten, no text artifacts, and strong readability as a small app category cover. Regenerate if any check fails.
```
